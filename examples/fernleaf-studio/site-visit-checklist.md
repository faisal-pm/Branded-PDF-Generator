## Before the visit

- [x] Confirm access times with the homeowner
- [x] Print the survey plan and planting schedule
- [ ] Charge the laser level and camera
- [ ] Pack soil test kit and sample bags

> [!WARNING]
> The rear boundary wall is unstable. Stay at least two metres from it until the
> structural engineer has signed it off.

## On site

1. Walk the boundary with the homeowner and note any changes since the survey.
2. Photograph each bed from the marked viewpoints.
3. Take soil samples from beds A, C and F.
4. Mark existing plants to keep with orange tape.

> [!TIP]
> Morning light shows drainage problems best. Look for standing water near the
> patio before 10am.
